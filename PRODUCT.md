# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

PHP (CodeIgniter Framework), Bootstrap 5.3, Vanilla CSS (LINE Seed Sans TH), Three.js 3D WebGL, FontAwesome 6.4

## Users

Primary Users: Individual residents and small enterprise operators in Nonthaburi Province, Thailand, who require accessible, fast, transparent, and legal micro-loans (PICO Finance) for personal or business liquidity.

## Product Purpose

Infinite Fintech serves as a licensed, legitimate, transparent, and fair provincial retail loan provider (PICO Finance) regulated by the Ministry of Finance. It aims to provide easy access to legal funding, eliminate predatory unofficial loans, and promote financial literacy and liquidity.

## Positioning

A fully licensed PICO Finance institution operating directly under the supervision of the Ministry of Finance, offering clear effective-rate interest (up to 35.80% APR), 30-minute automated loan approvals, 24/7 mobile disbursement, and zero hidden fees.

## Operating Context

Customers apply online via the mobile application (Ounjai Cash / อุ่นใจแคช) or review terms on the web portal. The web application provides product information, loan eligibility criteria, calculation previews, application guides, license verification, and legal governance policies.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Capabilities**: Multi-language support (Thai & English), Interactive 3D WebGL interface, Effective Rate loan calculator preview, 7-step application workflow, PDPA privacy & legal terms viewer, license verification portal link.
- **Service Area Constraint**: Exclusively serves residents in Nonthaburi Province, Thailand.
- **Credit Limits**: Maximum 50,000 THB limit per loan, repayment terms 91–180 days, interest capped at 35.80% APR (Effective Rate).
